**Roomba Rights of SPLORR!!**

*A metaphor about trying to nap. In SPLORR!!*

You are a cat. The house has places to nap. The house also has a robot vacuum.

You can't see where the vacuum is going. You can only watch it, and it moves two tiles for every one of yours.

**How to play**

- **Arrow keys or WASD** to move. That's the only input.
- The game is **turn based**. Nothing happens until you move, and then the vacuum moves.
- **Bumping a wall** keeps you where you are and uses your turn. It's the only way to wait.
- Walk onto a **nap spot** (sofa, basket or keyboard) and bump a wall three times in a row to finish a nap. Every spot sits against a wall.
- Nap in every spot to settle the house. There are five houses, and then they start over.
- **Touching the vacuum costs a life.** You have 3, you can hold up to 9, and you earn one for every 20 naps.
- The vacuum's route is hidden, and it never changes. Learn it.
- Keys you press while the vacuum is moving are remembered and played on your next turns.

**Made for Jamference: AI Game Jam Hack 1**

Theme: Cat and Robot. Restriction: Movement Input Only.

**AI use**

Built with Claude Code (Claude Sonnet 5.5). The AI wrote all the game code (Odin, compiled to WebAssembly), the web wrapper, the build setup, the five level layouts and the cover image generator. It also wrote the tests, including a solver that checks every level can be cleared without being caught.

I was the product owner: I chose the concept, made the design decisions together with the AI (the 2-tile vacuum, the hidden route, the lives and levels, the look of each house) and playtested every build as QA. I wrote no code.
