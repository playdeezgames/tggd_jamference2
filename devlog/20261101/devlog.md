# Roomba Rights of SPLORR!! now makes noise

Title: Roomba Rights of SPLORR!!: sound, a mute key and a footer

The cat can be heard now. The jam build had no sound at all; this update adds nine short effects:

- a soft blip for every step the cat takes
- a low tick for each of the two tiles the vacuum moves, so you can hear it coming even when you cannot see where it is going
- two rising notes as the cat settles into a nap spot, then a chime when the nap is done
- a harsh drop when the vacuum gets you
- a little arpeggio for an extra life, a fanfare for a cleared house, and a sad descending tune when the lives run out

The sounds were written in the Anvil of SPLORR!!, as short melodies in old BASIC PLAY notation, and exported as WAV files. Nothing plays until your first key press, and the pitch of the step and vacuum sounds wobbles a little so repeats do not sound identical.

**M** turns the sound on and off, and the game remembers your choice. It is the only key that is not movement, so it bends the jam's "Movement Input Only" restriction a little. The jam is over, and this seemed more useful than a game you cannot silence.

Also new: a footer under the game with credits, the AI-use line and links back to itch.io and the rest of the games, and the same build now runs on GitHub Pages, where the latest version always lives: https://playdeezgames.github.io/tggd_jamference2/. The source is at https://github.com/playdeezgames/tggd_jamference2.

The levels, rules and look are unchanged.
